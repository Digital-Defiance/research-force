# Requirements Document

## Introduction

**Product and installation scope.** The reusable software product is named **Research Force** and is licensed under MIT. ResearchForce.org is its first installation. Other teams can operate independent installations of the same software; a hosted multi-tenant service is outside this scope. The domains, founding team, first Project, organizational status, and public policy statements below describe the ResearchForce.org installation. Other installations supply their own identity and policy configuration without changing the shared privacy, authorization, attribution, and publication rules.

The software licence covers software and development documentation, not research artifacts or other instance content. Each instance runner or owner chooses the Content_Licence for that instance, subject to author agreements and third-party rights. ResearchForce.org's Content_Licence remains undecided. Setup must require an explicit content-licence choice before collecting licence agreements or publishing research; MIT must never be substituted automatically. Private records, attachments, backups, credentials, and real personal data are not source distribution material.

ResearchForce (called the Research_CMS in this document) is a private, shared research workspace and the source of a public research archive, run at researchforce.org. ResearchForce is run by an informal, independent group of researchers, scientists, historians, and engineers. The group is not a company, is not incorporated, and is not a registered non-profit or charity; it is not run for profit and does the work for its own sake, to professional and scientific standards. Research is organized into Projects. The first Project, and the current focus, is the team's campaign to break the Kriegsmarine Enigma M4 message P1030680. The platform supports further Projects, and the home page can feature several of them. Inside, the Research_CMS replaces coordination over email and scattered files. Members confer in threaded discussions, submit findings, sources, compute runs and their logs, hypotheses, correspondence records, archival requests, journal entries, and discussion topics, and review each other's work. Every Member sees a "new research" flag on items they have not yet seen, and, by default, is notified in the app and by email when new results arrive.

**Group notes and log summaries.** When Members record a new communication or new research, they often write one message to the group and a different entry for the log, and what goes into the log, and how it is worded, has been disputed. A Member who submits a Research_Item can therefore write two separate texts: a Group_Note, the message to the team that used to be a group email, posted as the first Reply in the item's Thread; and a Log_Summary, the text recorded for the item in the Project_Log. The Log_Summary's author decides its wording. Any other Member can attach a signed Dissent_Note, which is shown next to the Log_Summary wherever Members see the Log_Summary. The Project_Log, a chronological view of Log_Summaries across all kinds of research in a Project, succeeds the hand-written journal. A Member can ask the platform's own AI service for an AI_Draft of a summary, which the Member edits or accepts before anything is saved.

The Public_Site is the primary public product: an open research archive that other researchers can browse, search, cite, and build on. The archive keeps everything the team has learned, including dead ends, eliminated hypotheses, and negative compute results, as part of the historical record. Outside researchers have no account and never see internal content. They contribute material, corrections, and comments through a moderated form on the Public_Site.

**Team and roles.** The founding team is three Members with different specialties: the campaign owner, a computer engineer who runs the mechanical and compute work (Administrator); a historian who leads the archival work and curates the archive (Administrator); and a historian and computer scientist who runs independent compute work (Contributor). There are two Roles. A Contributor adds and maintains research. An Administrator can do everything a Contributor can and also curates: publishing, moderation, membership, configuration, imports, and exports. Permissions are defined per Role in one place, so a Role can be added later without a redesign. Member profiles record specialties so that review requests and mentions can be addressed to a specialty as well as to a person.

The team values provenance and attribution. The record distinguishes exact quotes from labelled paraphrases, primary from secondary sources, and confirmed facts from source-reported claims. The Research_CMS preserves these distinctions instead of flattening them, including on the public site.

**Presentation and public policies.** The Public_Site is the face of a small research group that works to professional standards, so the Public_Site and the Workspace share one consistent, accessible design that works on phones and desktops, prints cleanly, and gives citation tools and link previews accurate metadata (Requirement 45). The visual identity itself is decided in design. The Public_Site also states who the group is and is not, how it works, how its work is licensed, what personal data it processes and who is responsible for that processing, and how anyone can ask for a correction or a removal (Requirement 46). Because the group is not a legal entity, copyright in published work stays with the credited authors. Members live in several countries, including EU member states, so data-protection expectations such as those of the GDPR apply to how the Research_CMS handles personal data. The campaign owner, who is the site's technical owner, is the person responsible for the Research_CMS and its processing of personal data, and the Privacy page names that person (the Responsible_Person, Requirement 46.35). This document records that context for the design; it is not legal advice.

**Privacy model.** Two separate settings control who sees what. The Access_Level controls who inside the team can read a Research_Item: Whole_Team, the default, under which every Member can read it, or Restricted, under which only its Owner, the Members on its Access_List, and Administrators can read it, together with everything under it (Requirement 47). A Restricted Topic gives a few Members a private discussion attached to the research, in place of direct messages. The Disclosure_Level controls what the public website shows, and a Restricted Research_Item is never published. Each Project, each item, each Entry_Section, each attachment, and each Dissent_Note carries one Disclosure_Level:

- **Public**: the Public_Site shows the item's public presentation and lists the item's attachments that are themselves Public.
- **Withheld**: the Public_Site shows only a Disclosure_Line (that the item exists, the parties, the date, the kind of material, and an optional short note such as "data received from X, archived"), with no content, summary, attachments, or downloads.
- **Not_Published**: the Public_Site shows no trace of the item.

An attachment's Disclosure_Level is independent of its parent's, so a Public correspondence record can carry a Withheld or Not_Published attachment. Nothing a Parent hides on the Public_Site can be exposed through a child. The Project sits at the top of every element's ancestors, so nothing in a Not_Published Project appears publicly, whatever its own Disclosure_Level. Only Administrators change disclosure, and an Administrator newly publishes another Member's work only with that Member's consent, given by the Member's own Publication_Request or by approving an Administrator's Publication_Proposal (Requirement 23.17 to 23.22). Rights and consent also gate publication: an attachment can be Public only when its reproduction rights permit publication, and a correspondence record can be Public only when every outside party has consented, with Withheld as the highest level allowed otherwise. Any Member can place a Confidentiality_Hold on an Item to keep it from being published; the hold is lifted by the Member who placed it or the Owner, or overridden only by two different Administrators (Requirements 48 and 49). The existing campaign journal and its attachments are migrated into the Research_CMS, and every imported entry, section, and attachment starts as Not_Published until an Administrator classifies each one explicitly. Material bulk-imported from the curator's collection and material accepted from outside researchers start the same way.

**Platform (fixed constraint).** The Research_CMS is custom-built on Cloudflare: a Hono API on Cloudflare Workers, D1 accessed through Drizzle ORM for data, R2 for files, a React and Vite user interface (the same stack as the existing journal site), Cloudflare Access for login, so the Research_CMS stores no passwords, and Workers AI for optional AI drafting. The Research_CMS is served under the researchforce.org domain. The detailed architecture, including the choice of AI model, is deferred to design. The requirements respect these free-plan facts (October 2026):

- Workers Free: 100,000 requests per day, 10 ms CPU time per request, 100 MB maximum request body, 50 D1 queries per Worker invocation, and 5 cron triggers per account. Static asset requests are free and unlimited; each static asset file is limited to 25 MiB.
- D1 Free: 500 MB per database and 5 GB per account (up to 10 databases), 5 million rows read and 100,000 rows written per day. Once a daily limit is reached, queries fail until the 00:00 UTC reset. Time Travel provides 7 days of point-in-time recovery. Workers Paid (USD 5 per month) lifts these daily caps.
- R2 Free: 10 GB-month of storage, 1 million Class A and 10 million Class B operations per month, and no egress fees. A payment method must be on file to activate R2; nothing is charged within the free allowance. Files above the 100 MB request body limit go straight to R2 through a signed URL.
- Cloudflare Access (Zero Trust) is free for up to 50 users, with login by email one-time PIN or Google or GitHub sign-in.
- Cloudflare Turnstile, the bot check for the public submission form, is free.
- Cloudflare Web Analytics, a visitor analytics service that uses no cookies or other browser storage and reports aggregate figures, including Core Web Vitals, is free.
- Workers AI: 10,000 Neurons per day are free on both the Workers Free and Workers Paid plans. Beyond that, Workers Paid charges USD 0.011 per 1,000 Neurons, and on Workers Free requests stop at the daily cap until 00:00 UTC. A summary of about 2,000 input tokens and 300 output tokens costs about 19 Neurons on Llama 3.1 8B (fp8-fast), so roughly 500 such summaries fit in the daily free allocation, or about 85 on a 70B model. Cloudflare states that it does not use content processed by Workers AI to train models and does not make that content available to other customers.
- Existing content to migrate: about 415 KB of journal Markdown and about 137 MB of attachments, the largest a 72 MB PDF. The curator's collected material will be bulk-imported as well; its size is not yet known.

**Email (Microsoft 365).** The Research_CMS sends every email, to Members and to Submitters, through the Microsoft Graph sendMail API from one dedicated sending mailbox at researchforce.org (the Sending_Address) in the campaign owner's existing Microsoft 365 tenant. As of October 2026, Exchange Online lets each mailbox send to 10,000 recipients in a 24-hour sliding window and send 30 messages per minute, and it throttles submissions above that rate and carries them over instead of rejecting them. Each tenant also has a Tenant External Recipient Rate Limit (TERRL), the tenant's shared external limit: a daily number of external recipients that scales with the number of licences in the tenant and that every mailbox in the tenant shares. The Exchange admin center's "Tenant Outbound External Recipients" report shows the tenant's usage ([Microsoft's sending-limits documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-office-365/outbound-spam-sending-limits-troubleshoot)). Microsoft had planned a Mailbox External Recipient Rate Limit of 2,000 external recipients per mailbox per 24 hours and cancelled it in January 2026 ([Microsoft's cancellation notice](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/exchange/exchange-online-canceling-the-mailbox-external-recipient-rate-limit/4483498)). Most Members' addresses and every Submitter's address are external, so the Research_CMS's email counts against the tenant's shared external limit. The Research_CMS therefore applies its own safety cap, the Recipient_Cap, to the number of recipients it addresses in any 24 hours (Requirement 20.16), and the cap is kept well below the 10,000-recipient mailbox limit and below the tenant's shared external limit, which for a small tenant can be low. The Recipient_Cap starts at 2,000, and an Administrator can lower or raise it within 1 to 10,000, so that no setting exceeds the mailbox limit (Requirement 20.25). Before launch, the campaign owner checks the tenant's shared external limit and sets the Recipient_Cap below it (assumption 47). The planned load, about 100 emails to Members per day (the Expected_Usage_Profile) plus the acknowledgements and outcomes for at most 20 Outside_Submissions per day, is well within the mailbox limits and the initial Recipient_Cap, and Exchange Online reaches arbitrary recipients, which emails to Submitters need. The team expects to start with 3 to 4 Members who each receive 1 to 3 emails per day; the Expected_Usage_Profile and the limit of 20 Outside_Submissions per day are planning limits, not forecasts. Requirement 20 keeps the Research_CMS within those limits, restricts the Research_CMS's credentials to sending from the Sending_Address, keeps those credentials out of code and data, and requires mail that passes SPF and DKIM checks for researchforce.org (Requirement 20.15 to 20.25). Where a requirement describes a capability, it keeps the general term Email_Provider, so that changing provider later needs no redesign (Requirement 20.11). Options considered and not chosen: Cloudflare Email Service reaches arbitrary recipients only on Workers Paid (USD 5 per month with 3,000 emails included, then USD 0.35 per 1,000); Amazon SES costs cents per thousand emails; SendGrid no longer has a free plan.

**Cost.** Per-member new-research flags and notifications add D1 writes. Public search, the public submission form, and exports add Worker requests, D1 reads, and storage. AI drafting uses Workers AI Neurons and stays inside the daily free allocation, so it is never charged. Email goes through the campaign owner's existing Microsoft 365 subscription, which exists independently of the Research_CMS, so email adds no platform charge under Requirement 28.4 and the Research_CMS can stay on the Workers Free plan. The Policy_Pages, including the People page, page metadata, and the design's fonts, style sheets, and images are delivered as stored files or from cache, so they add no D1 queries, and the assets add no Worker requests (Requirement 28.16). Requirement 28 re-checks the cost goal against the larger scope and keeps public traffic from using up the free-plan limits that Member operations depend on.

This document covers the requirements phase only. Requirements 1 to 46 keep their earlier numbers and criterion numbers, and criteria added in this revision follow each requirement's existing criteria: Requirement 20 gains criteria 15 to 25 for sending email through Microsoft 365, and Requirement 46 gains criteria 35 to 40 for the Responsible_Person.

**Revision of October 2026 (team privacy and publication consent).** After the founding team reviewed the open questions, this revision adds: restricted access inside the team (Requirement 47, with Requirement 2.8 narrowed accordingly); Confidentiality_Holds (Requirement 48); approval by two different Administrators for the few actions that override a Member's protection (Requirement 49); Research_Questions as curated ways into the public archive (Requirement 50, a new Research_Item kind); the Owner's consent to publication (Requirement 23.17 to 23.22); moving an Attachment to another Parent (Requirement 12.12 to 12.15); importing journal attachments that no entry links to into an Unassigned_Attachments_Source instead of failing them (Requirement 26.2, 26.12, and 26.15); a yearly review of Submitter contact details (Requirement 32.19 to 32.22); and Consent_Requests, emails through which people in a correspondence and holders of rights in a document give or refuse consent to publication with one click and no account (Requirement 51). The Disclosure_Level formerly called Private is now called Not_Published, so that it is not read as "visible only to me". Decisions, provisional assumptions, open questions, and proposed capabilities to confirm are listed in the final section.

## Glossary

- **Research_CMS**: The Research Force software platform, including the Public_Site. This document specifies its first installation, ResearchForce.org, with the installation-specific context identified in the Introduction. The named services below are logical capabilities of the Research_CMS, not a prescribed architecture.
- **Project**: A top-level container of Research_Items with a name of 1 to 100 characters that differs, ignoring letter case, from every other Project's name; a short description of 1 to 500 characters; a Project_Slug; a featured flag; a position in the Project_Order; an optional Project_Accent; and a Disclosure_Level. Every Research_Item belongs to exactly one Project, and an Attachment, Entry_Section, Reply, or Dissent_Note belongs to the Project of its Root_Item. The first Project is the P1030680 Project, for the campaign to break the message P1030680.
- **Project_Slug**: The stable, human-readable identifier of a Project used in the addresses of the Project's Project_Section: 1 to 40 characters, each a lowercase letter a to z, a digit, or a hyphen, starting with a letter or digit, unique among all Projects, and fixed once the Project's Effective_Disclosure first becomes Public or Withheld.
- **Project_Order**: The single display order of all Projects, in which each Project holds a different position.
- **Featured_Project**: A Project whose featured flag is on, which the Public_Site home page presents while the Project's Effective_Disclosure is Public or Withheld (Requirement 40.1).
- **Project_Section**: The part of the Public_Site that presents one Project whose Effective_Disclosure is Public: the Project's page, the Project's listings and public Project_Log, the Project's listing of Disclosure_Lines, and public search limited to the Project.
- **Project_Accent**: An accent colour that an Administrator chooses for a Project from the Design_System's set of accent colours, used in place of the Design_System's default accent only within the Project's Project_Section and on the public pages of the Project's Research_Items and Attachments (Requirement 45.3). A Project has no Project_Accent until an Administrator sets one.
- **Muted_Project**: A Project that a Member has muted, so that the Member receives no New_Submission, Reply, or Requirement 19.2 Item_Update Notification_Events concerning only that Project (Requirement 39.9).
- **Workspace**: Every page, file, and API resource of the Research_CMS other than the Public_Site. Forms opened by a Response_Link belong to the Public_Site.
- **Member**: A person who holds an account in the Research_CMS, in any Member_Status.
- **Member_Status**: A Member's account state, exactly one of Pending (from an invitation until the Member's first authentication after that invitation), Active, or Revoked (from revocation until the Member's email address is invited again). In this document, "active Member" means a Member whose Member_Status is Active, and "pending Member" means a Member whose Member_Status is Pending.
- **Role**: The named set of Permissions assigned to a Member. Each Member holds exactly one Role. The initial Roles are Contributor and Administrator.
- **Permission**: A named action that the Access_Control_Service allows or denies, such as creating an Item or changing a Disclosure_Level.
- **Role_Permission_Map**: The single definition that lists, for each Role, the Permissions the Role grants.
- **Contributor**: A Role whose Permissions are exactly those that Requirement 2.4 lists: to create Research_Items of every kind; upload Attachments; post Replies; add Cross_Links and remove the Cross_Links the Member added; create Review_Requests, including Publication_Requests; mark as Reviewed each Review_Request for which the Member is an Eligible_Reviewer; turn on the Do_Not_Name flag of any External_Party; edit the Member's own Profile; edit, correct, and withdraw the Items the Member owns and edit and correct the Entry_Sections of those Items, including writing and changing the Log_Summary of each Research_Item the Member owns; attach Dissent_Notes to the Log_Summaries of Research_Items that other Members own, and edit and withdraw the Dissent_Notes the Member wrote; and request AI_Drafts of the Log_Summaries of the Research_Items the Member owns or is submitting; together with the Permissions that Requirements 23.17 to 23.22, 47, and 48 grant to every active Member.
- **Administrator**: A Role whose Permissions are exactly those that Requirement 2.5 lists: every Contributor Permission, plus the Permissions to edit, correct, withdraw, and permanently delete any Item and edit and correct any Entry_Section, except the Log_Summary of a Research_Item that another Member owns; permanently delete any Dissent_Note; create Projects, change any Project's name, short description, Project_Slug, featured flag, Project_Accent, and position in the Project_Order, and move any Research_Item to another Project; remove any Cross_Link; turn off the Do_Not_Name flag of any External_Party; set or change any Disclosure_Level, including a Project's, and any Public_Presentation, Public_Summary, or Disclosure_Line; request AI_Drafts of any Public_Summary or Disclosure_Line; make Moderation_Decisions on Outside_Submissions; invite people, change any Member's Role, and revoke any Member's access; add Roles to the Role_Permission_Map and change the Permissions of added Roles; add, rename, and retire values in every configurable value set, mark each Rights_Status value as permitting or not permitting publication, and write the Public_Description of each Methods_Term; edit the Policy_Sections of every Policy_Page; change every workspace-wide configurable setting; run the journal and attachment migration and Bulk_Imports; request and delete Public_Archive_Exports; and request, download, restore, and delete Internal_Backups. An Administrator holds no Permission to edit another Member's Profile, to write or change the Log_Summary of a Research_Item that another Member owns, or to edit or withdraw a Dissent_Note that another Member wrote. Administrators also hold the Permissions that Requirements 23.17 to 23.22, 47, 48, 49, and 51 grant to Administrators, including sending Consent_Requests and recording or erasing Contact_Emails, and none that would let one Administrator alone widen another Member's Access_List, override a Confidentiality_Hold, or publish another Member's work without that Member's consent.
- **Owner**: The Member who created an Item; for imported content, the Administrator who ran the import; for an Item created from an Outside_Submission, the Administrator who accepted the Outside_Submission. The Owner of a Research_Item is the author of the Research_Item's Log_Summary.
- **Credit**: The people named as authors of an Item: initially the Owner, except that the Credit of an Item created from an Outside_Submission is the Submitter, and the Credit of an imported Attachment whose source journal or Import_Manifest gives a crediting contributor is that crediting contributor (Requirements 26.2, 37.1, and 37.12). Wherever the Research_CMS shows a Credit, including on the Public_Site, in Citation_Lines, and in citation metadata, each Member named in the Credit appears under the Member's display name.
- **Profile**: A Member's display name, Specialties, affiliations, short biography, time zone, Notification_Preferences, Public_Profile setting, and Licence_Agreement.
- **Public_Profile**: The presentation of a Member on the People page, made of the Member's display name, Specialties, affiliations, and short biography, which the Public_Site shows only while the Member's Public_Profile setting is On, a setting that is initially Off and that only the Member can change (Requirement 46).
- **Licence_Agreement**: A Member's dated statement, given by the Member on the Member's own Profile, that the Member's own work presented on the Public_Site is licensed under the Content_Licence named in the statement. A Member has no Licence_Agreement until the Member gives one.
- **Specialty**: A field of expertise drawn from a configurable set whose initial values are Historian, Archival_Research, Cryptanalysis, Computer_Science, and Computer_Engineering.
- **Identity_Provider**: Cloudflare Access, the external login service that verifies a person's identity by email one-time PIN or Google or GitHub sign-in.
- **Authentication_Service**: The logical capability that authenticates Members through the Identity_Provider and establishes sessions.
- **Access_Control_Service**: The logical capability that enforces Role Permissions, Member_Status, and Access_Levels on every request.
- **Access_Level**: The setting of a Research_Item that controls who inside the team can read the Research_Item and every element whose Root_Item it is: exactly one of Whole_Team or Restricted, initially Whole_Team (Requirement 47). Projects carry no Access_Level.
- **Whole_Team**: The Access_Level under which every active Member can read the Research_Item.
- **Restricted**: The Access_Level under which only the Research_Item's Owner, the Members on its Access_List, and Administrators can read the Research_Item and every element whose Root_Item it is.
- **Access_List**: The list of Members, other than the Owner, who can read a Restricted Research_Item. Administrators can read every Restricted Research_Item whether or not they are on its Access_List.
- **Can_Read**: A Member Can_Read an element when the Member is active and the Access_Level of the element's Root_Item is Whole_Team, or the Member is the Root_Item's Owner, is on its Access_List, or is an Administrator.
- **Records_Service**: The logical capability that creates, edits, and organizes Research_Items and Entry_Sections.
- **Research_Item**: A top-level record of one of these kinds: Journal_Entry, Finding, Source, Run, Hypothesis, Correspondence, Archival_Request, Topic, or Research_Question. Every Research_Item has a title of 1 to 200 characters, an Access_Level, and belongs to exactly one Project.
- **Item**: A Research_Item, an Attachment, or a Reply.
- **Parent**: The Research_Item, Reply, or Attachment that directly contains an Entry_Section, Attachment, Reply, or Dissent_Note. The ancestors of an element are its chain of Parents up to the Root_Item, followed by the Root_Item's Project; the only ancestor of a Research_Item is its Project, and a Project has no ancestors.
- **Root_Item**: For a Research_Item, the Research_Item itself; for an Attachment, Reply, Entry_Section, or Dissent_Note, the Research_Item at the top of the element's chain of Parents.
- **Journal_Entry**: A Research_Item recording one phase or sub-phase of the campaign, made of Entry_Sections: one or more for a Journal_Entry that a Member submits, and zero or more for a Journal_Entry that the Journal_Parser creates, such as the Journal_Entry for a Phase_Heading followed directly by the Phase_Heading of a sub-phase.
- **Entry_Section**: A distinct part of a Journal_Entry, such as a paragraph, list item, or ledger row, that carries a Disclosure_Level independent of the Journal_Entry.
- **Campaign_Status**: The status of a Journal_Entry, drawn from a configurable set whose initial values are COMPLETE, SUSPENDED, PARTIAL, STOPPED, OPEN, ELIMINATED, and CLEAN_NEGATIVE.
- **Finding**: A Research_Item recording a title, a claim, the evidence for the claim, a Finding_Status, and a Scope_Caveat; Findings together form the claim and evidence ledger.
- **Finding_Status**: The status of a Finding, drawn from a configurable set whose initial values are Confirmed, Open, Withdrawn, and Superseded.
- **Scope_Caveat**: A statement of what a Finding's claim covers and does not cover.
- **Source**: A Research_Item describing a research source or document and the source's provenance.
- **Source_Kind**: The classification of a Source as Primary or Secondary.
- **Reliability_Scale**: A configurable set of reliability ratings for Sources whose initial values are High, Medium, Low, and Unknown.
- **Rights_Status**: The reproduction-rights status of a Source or Attachment, drawn from a configurable set whose initial values are Own_Work, Public_Domain, Licensed, Permission_Granted, Unknown, and Restricted. Each value is marked as permitting or not permitting publication; initially Own_Work, Public_Domain, Licensed, and Permission_Granted permit publication.
- **Own_Work**: The Rights_Status of material created by Members or Submitters and published under the Content_Licence.
- **Run**: A Research_Item recording one compute run: title, scope, parameters, tool name and tool version, compute resource, times, counts, Run_Outcome, Resume_Point, and log files.
- **Run_Outcome**: The outcome of a Run, drawn from a configurable set whose initial values are In_Progress, Complete, Clean_Negative, Partial, Suspended, and Stopped.
- **Resume_Point**: The recorded position from which an unfinished Run can be continued.
- **Hypothesis**: A Research_Item recording a title, a statement of a candidate explanation or key-space assumption, and a Hypothesis_Status.
- **Hypothesis_Status**: The status of a Hypothesis, drawn from a configurable set whose initial values are Open and Eliminated.
- **Elimination_Scope**: A statement of exactly which part of a Hypothesis a negative result rules out.
- **Correspondence**: A Research_Item recording one communication between Parties, with title, date, channel, direction, content, a Consent_Status for each External_Party, and Attachments.
- **Party**: A person or organization taking part in a Correspondence or Archival_Request, or named as the person who recovered a Source, either a Member or a named external party.
- **External_Party**: A Party who is not a Member, with an optional Contact_Email.
- **Contact_Email**: An External_Party's email address, used only to send Consent_Requests, shown only to Members, and never presented on the Public_Site or in a Public_Archive_Export.
- **Consent_Status**: An External_Party's consent to publication of one Correspondence's content: exactly one of Not_Asked, Requested, Granted, or Refused.
- **Do_Not_Name**: A flag on an External_Party under which the Public_Site shows the External_Party's Generic_Label instead of the External_Party's name.
- **Generic_Label**: The neutral description, such as "an archive" or "a researcher", shown in place of the name of an External_Party marked Do_Not_Name.
- **Archival_Request**: A Research_Item tracking a request to an archive: the title, the archive or desk, contact, date sent, channel, request text, Archival_Request_Status, and reply.
- **Archival_Request_Status**: The status of an Archival_Request, drawn from a configurable set whose initial values are Planned, Sent, Replied, and Closed.
- **Follow_Up_Interval**: The period after which a Sent Archival_Request without a reply becomes Follow_Up_Due: a whole number of days from 1 to 365, initially 30, configurable by an Administrator.
- **Follow_Up_Due**: The condition of an Archival_Request that is in the Sent status, is not withdrawn, and has a date sent at least one Follow_Up_Interval before the current date in the Owner's time zone.
- **Topic**: A Research_Item that starts a general discussion not attached to another Item.
- **Research_Question**: A Research_Item that states one question the research is trying to answer, with an introduction and Question_Groups that lead a reader to the Research_Items and Attachments that bear on it (Requirement 50).
- **Question_Group**: A titled, ordered list of links from a Research_Question to Research_Items and Attachments of the same Project, such as "The story so far", "Evidence", "Hypotheses tested", or "Dead ends".
- **Quote**: Content labelled as a source's exact wording, with a named source.
- **Paraphrase**: Content labelled as a restatement of a source in other words, with a named source.
- **Source_Reported**: A label marking content as a claim reported by a source and not yet confirmed by the team.
- **Attachment**: A file stored by the Research_CMS together with the file's metadata, belonging to exactly one Parent.
- **Attachment_Service**: The logical capability that uploads, stores, serves, previews, and versions Attachments.
- **Object_Store**: The file storage backend (R2) for Attachments, Outside_Submission files, and export packages.
- **Origin**: A provenance note on an Attachment describing where the file came from and the file's reliability.
- **Unassigned_Attachments_Source**: The one Source per Project, titled "Unassigned legacy attachments", that the journal and attachment import creates when needed and uses as the Parent of each imported attachment file that no imported Journal_Entry links to, so that the file is kept with its provenance recorded as unknown until a Member moves it to a better Parent (Requirements 12.12 and 26.15).
- **Direct_Upload_Threshold**: The file size, configurable and at most 100 MB, above which an upload goes to the Object_Store without passing through a Worker request body.
- **Attachment_Size_Limit**: The largest file the Attachment_Service accepts from a Member, initially 1 GB.
- **Version_Chain**: The ordered sequence of Attachments in which each later Attachment supersedes the one before it.
- **Protected_File**: The file of an Attachment whose Effective_Disclosure is Withheld or Not_Published; any file uploaded through the Outside_Submission_Form or a Response_Link that has not become an Attachment file; or any copy, preview, or rendition that the Attachment_Service makes of such a file.
- **Download_Link**: A link issued to one authenticated active Member that delivers one Protected_File, one file of a Public_Archive_Export, or one file of an Internal_Backup without login, and expires 15 minutes after issue.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Member Authentication

**User Story:** As a team member, I want to log in with my own identity, so that only authorized people reach the workspace and the Research_CMS keeps no passwords.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a person who has no session opens any page or file of the Workspace, THE Authentication_Service SHALL send the person to the Identity_Provider's login and, once the person has a session, open the page or file the person requested.
2. THE Research_CMS SHALL verify Member identity only through the Identity_Provider and keep no password, password hash, or one-time PIN for any person.
3. WHEN a request for the Workspace reaches the Research_CMS at any of the Research_CMS's web addresses, THE Authentication_Service SHALL verify that the request carries an unexpired identity assertion signed by the Identity_Provider for the Research_CMS before the request reads or changes any data.
4. IF the email address verified by the Identity_Provider is not, ignoring letter case, the registered email address of a Member whose Member_Status is Pending or Active, THEN THE Access_Control_Service SHALL reject the request with an authorization error, establish no session, and change no data.
5. IF a request for an API resource of the Workspace carries no identity assertion, or an identity assertion that fails verification for any reason, including the Identity_Provider being unreachable, THEN THE Access_Control_Service SHALL reject the request with an authentication error and change no data.
6. WHEN a person authenticates through the Identity_Provider with the registered email address, ignoring letter case, of a Member whose Member_Status is Pending or Active, THE Authentication_Service SHALL establish a session bound to exactly that one Member.
7. WHEN a request in a session creates or changes data, THE Research_CMS SHALL record the session's Member, and never a Member named in the request, as the acting Member of the change, such as the Owner of a new Item or the editing Member of a Revision.
8. WHEN 24 hours have passed since a session was established, THE Authentication_Service SHALL end the session, so that the next request for the Workspace from that browser requires authentication through the Identity_Provider.
9. WHEN a Member chooses to log out, THE Authentication_Service SHALL end the Member's session in that browser, so that the next request for the Workspace from that browser requires authentication through the Identity_Provider.

### Requirement 2: Members, Roles, and Permissions

**User Story:** As an administrator, I want two roles, one for adding research and one for curating everything, defined in one place, so that the team can contribute and publish safely and we can add roles later without a redesign.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an Administrator invites a person by an email address that belongs to no Member or to a Member whose Member_Status is Pending or Revoked, THE Research_CMS SHALL set the Member who holds that address, or a new Member for that address if none holds it, to Member_Status Pending and the Role the Administrator selects, and send an invitation email to that address within 2 minutes.
2. THE Research_CMS SHALL assign each Member exactly one Role defined in the Role_Permission_Map, exactly one Member_Status from the set {Pending, Active, Revoked}, and one email address that no other Member holds, with addresses that differ only in letter case counted as the same address.
3. THE Access_Control_Service SHALL decide every permission check from the Role_Permission_Map and the requesting Member's Role and Member_Status as they stand when the request is received, including for requests in sessions established before a change to any of them.
4. THE Access_Control_Service SHALL grant Members holding the Contributor Role the Permissions to create Research_Items of every kind; upload Attachments; post Replies; add Cross_Links and remove the Cross_Links the Member added; create Review_Requests, including Publication_Requests; mark as Reviewed each Review_Request for which the Member is an Eligible_Reviewer; turn on the Do_Not_Name flag of any External_Party; edit the Member's own Profile; edit, correct, and withdraw the Items the Member owns and edit and correct the Entry_Sections of those Items, including writing and changing the Log_Summary of each Research_Item the Member owns; attach Dissent_Notes to the Log_Summaries of Research_Items that other Members own, and edit and withdraw the Dissent_Notes the Member wrote; and request AI_Drafts of the Log_Summaries of the Research_Items the Member owns or is submitting, where adding a new version of an Attachment counts as editing that Attachment and restoring an earlier Revision counts as editing the Item or Entry_Section concerned; together with the Permissions that Requirements 23.17 to 23.22, 47, and 48 grant to every active Member.
5. THE Access_Control_Service SHALL grant Members holding the Administrator Role every Contributor Permission and the Permissions to edit, correct, withdraw, and permanently delete any Item and edit and correct any Entry_Section, except the Log_Summary of a Research_Item that another Member owns; permanently delete any Dissent_Note; create Projects, change any Project's name, short description, Project_Slug, featured flag, Project_Accent, and position in the Project_Order, and move any Research_Item to another Project; remove any Cross_Link; turn off the Do_Not_Name flag of any External_Party; set or change any Disclosure_Level, including a Project's, and any Public_Presentation, Public_Summary, or Disclosure_Line; request AI_Drafts of any Public_Summary or Disclosure_Line; make Moderation_Decisions on Outside_Submissions; invite people, change any Member's Role, and revoke any Member's access; add Roles to the Role_Permission_Map and change the Permissions of added Roles; add, rename, and retire values in every configurable value set, mark each Rights_Status value as permitting or not permitting publication, and write the Public_Description of each Methods_Term; edit the Policy_Sections of every Policy_Page; change every workspace-wide configurable setting, including the Follow_Up_Interval, Email_Batch_Window, Direct_Upload_Threshold, Public_Upload_Size_Limit, Outside_Submission_Limits, Public_Traffic_Budget, configured Email_Provider, Recipient_Cap, AI_Drafting_Setting, AI_Input_Limit, and designation of the Responsible_Person; run the journal and attachment migration and Bulk_Imports; request and delete Public_Archive_Exports; and request, download, restore, and delete Internal_Backups; together with the Permissions that Requirements 23.17 to 23.22, 47, 48, 49, and 51 grant to Administrators, including sending Consent_Requests and recording or erasing Contact_Emails; but no Permission to edit another Member's Profile, to write or change the Log_Summary of a Research_Item that another Member owns, or to edit or withdraw a Dissent_Note that another Member wrote.
6. WHEN a Role is added to the Role_Permission_Map with Permissions drawn from those the existing Roles grant, THE Research_CMS SHALL enforce exactly those Permissions for Members holding the new Role and offer the new Role wherever an Administrator selects a Role, with no change to the Research_CMS outside the Role_Permission_Map.
7. IF a Member requests an action that the Member's Role does not grant, including a Restoration that would change a Disclosure_Level, Public_Presentation, Public_Summary, or Disclosure_Line, THEN THE Access_Control_Service SHALL reject the whole request without changing any data and return an authorization error.
8. WHEN a Member completes a Submission that adds a new version of an existing Attachment, THE Attachment_Service SHALL create the new version as a new Attachment that supersedes the existing Attachment and belongs to the existing Attachment's Parent, and place the new version last in the existing Attachment's Version_Chain, starting a new Version_Chain of the two Attachments if the existing Attachment has no earlier version.
2. THE Attachment_Service SHALL leave a superseded Attachment's file, metadata, Item_Identifier, Disclosure_Level, and every Cross_Link to or from the superseded Attachment unchanged by the supersession, and allow Members to open, preview, download, and add Cross_Links to the superseded Attachment in the same ways as an Attachment that is not superseded.

7. IF a Member submits a new version of an existing Attachment that a later Attachment already supersedes at the time of submission, THEN THE Attachment_Service SHALL keep the Submission unsubmitted and the Version_Chain unchanged, identify the last Attachment in the Version_Chain, and offer the Member the option to submit the file as a new version of that last Attachment instead.

THE Attachment_Service SHALL deliver every rendering or extract made from an Attachment file or Outside_Submission file for a preview, such as a reduced image or a text excerpt, only to authenticated active Members, either in response to the Member's own request or through a link issued to that Member that expires within 15 minutes.

### Requirement 20: Email Notifications

**User Story:** As a team member, I want near-immediate email, with one email for everything that comes out of a single correspondence or submission, so that I am told promptly without a flood of messages.

WHEN a recipient's Daily_Digest hour arrives in the time zone in the recipient's Profile, THE Notification_Service SHALL send that recipient, within 15 minutes, one Daily_Digest email that lists, grouped by Root_Item, every Notification_Event for the recipient that occurred before that hour under a Daily_Digest email preference for the event's Event_Type and that no earlier Daily_Digest email listed.

6. THE Notification_Service SHALL limit the description of each Item in an email, including in the email's subject line, to the Item's Public_View at the time the email is sent, the Item's kind, the Event_Type, the acting Member's display name, the event time in the recipient's time zone, and a link that requires login, and the description of each Outside_Submission to the Event_Type, the Submission_Type, the event time in the recipient's time zone, for a Reminder under Requirement 46.25 the number of days since the Outside_Submission's receipt, and a link that requires login.

15. THE Research_CMS SHALL make at most 30 send attempts through the Email_Provider in any period of 60 seconds, counting every send attempt, including each retry, of every email that the Research_CMS sends: invitation emails (Requirement 2.1), emails that contain Notification_Events, and emails to Submitters (Requirements 31 and 32).
16. 18. THE Research_CMS SHALL apply criteria 8 to 11 and Requirements 2.14 and 32.12 to every email and retry held in the sending queue of criterion 17, and measure each time limit for sending an email in criteria 3, 4, and 9 and Requirements 2.1, 31.5, and 32.12 without the time that the email or retry spent in that queue.

THE Research_CMS SHALL use for the Email_Provider only credentials whose sole permission is to send email from the Sending_Address, so that the Email_Provider refuses each request made with those credentials to send email from any other address or mailbox or to read, change, or delete any mailbox or directory data.
20. THE Research_CMS SHALL keep the credentials for the Email_Provider only as secrets of the Cloudflare platform, such as Workers secrets, and each access token obtained with them only in the memory of the Worker that obtained it, and include neither the credentials nor any such access token in source code, a D1 database, the Object_Store, a log, a page, an email, a Public_Archive_Export, or an Internal_Backup.
21. WHEN an Administrator replaces the credentials for the Email_Provider by changing those platform secrets, such as before a client secret expires, THE Research_CMS SHALL make every send attempt that starts after the change, including retries and attempts for emails held in the sending queue, with the replacement credentials, without any change to the Research_CMS's source code or data.
22. THE Research_CMS SHALL send every email with the Sending_Address as the sender, so that, when the email is delivered directly to a receiving mail server that checks SPF and DKIM, the email passes an SPF check and a DKIM check that are each aligned with researchforce.org as DMARC (RFC 7489) defines alignment.
23. THE Research_CMS SHALL operate with a DMARC policy record for researchforce.org published in the domain's DNS, with the policy none, quarantine, or reject.
24. THE Research_CMS SHALL send every email through the Email_Provider without saving a copy in the mailbox of the Sending_Address, so that the mailbox keeps no copy of any email that the Research_CMS sends, such as an email containing a Submitter's email address that Requirement 32.8 or 46.26 later erases.
25. IF an Administrator sets the Recipient_Cap to a value other than a whole number from 1 to 10,000, THEN THE Research_CMS SHALL reject the value, keep the current Recipient_Cap, and show the allowed range.

### Requirement 21: Search

**User Story:** As a researcher, I want full-text search across all research content and attachment metadata, so that I can find earlier work, sources, and discussions in seconds.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Search_Service SHALL index every Item and Entry_Section, including withdrawn Items and superseded Attachments and excluding the Items of a Submission that has not completed, covering the title, Item_Identifier, and every free-text or name field of each Research_Item (for example a claim, Scope_Caveat, Party name, archival reference, or tool name), the text of each Entry_Section and Reply, and the title, Item_Identifier, caption, Origin note, and file name of each Attachment, but not the contents of Attachment files.
2. WHEN a Member submits a search query, THE Search_Service SHALL display the matching results in pages of at most 20, each page within 2 seconds of the Member's request while the indexed text totals at most 50 MB, or, when nothing matches, a message stating that no result matched.
3. THE Search_Service SHALL count an Item or Entry_Section as a match only when its indexed text contains every query term, with each word outside double quotation marks matching a whole word or the start of a word, regardless of letter case and diacritics, and with "ß" matching "ss", so that "Schlussel" matches "Schlüssel" and "SCHLÜSSELZETTEL" but not "SPRUCHSCHLÜSSEL".
4. THE Search_Service SHALL treat words enclosed in double quotation marks as one query term that matches only where those words occur as whole words in the same order with no other word between them, under the same letter-case, diacritic, and "ß" rules as other query terms.
5. THE Search_Service SHALL allow results to be filtered by the Research_Item kind of the result's Root_Item, a person named in the result's Credit, a Specialty currently held by a Member named in that Credit, the result's Disclosure_Level, and a range of creation dates that includes both end dates in the searching Member's time zone, and return only results that satisfy every applied filter, with each Entry_Section taking the Credit and creation time of its Journal_Entry.
6. WHEN a Submission or a Migration_Service import completes, or the History_Service records a Revision of an Item or Entry_Section, THE Search_Service SHALL, within 1 minute, match queries and filters only against the content and metadata as they stand after that event, so that removed text and permanently deleted Items no longer produce results.
7. THE Search_Service SHALL show each result with the result's kind; the result's own title or, for an Entry_Section or Reply, the Root_Item's title; the Credit; the creation date; a marker when the result is a withdrawn Item or a superseded Attachment; and an excerpt of at most 300 characters from the text where the match occurs that highlights the matched terms and keeps any Quote, Paraphrase, or Source_Reported label and named source on the excerpted text.
8. THE Search_Service SHALL rank matching results by relevance, placing every result whose own title contains every query term before all other results, and ordering results newest first by creation time within each of these two groups.
9. IF a Member submits a search query that contains no letter or digit or is longer than 200 characters, THEN THE Search_Service SHALL run no search, display a message indicating which of these conditions applies, and keep the query and filters as entered.
10. IF a search query fails, including because a platform daily limit has been reached, or no response arrives within 10 seconds of submission, THEN THE Search_Service SHALL display an error message indicating that search is unavailable, state that search resumes at 00:00 UTC when a daily limit is the cause, and keep the query and filters as entered.

### Requirement 22: Edit History, Corrections, and Withdrawals

**User Story:** As a historian, I want every correction and withdrawal recorded instead of silently overwritten, so that the record shows how our understanding changed.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. ### Requirement 27: Journal Markdown Parsing and Printing

**User Story:** As the campaign owner, I want the journal import to be checkable by printing the imported entries back to Markdown, so that I can confirm no content was lost or reordered.

2. WHEN the Journal_Parser receives journal Markdown that has top-level Markdown blocks before the first Phase_Heading, such as the existing journal's header facts table, summary paragraphs, and ledger table, THE Journal_Parser SHALL create one Journal_Entry without a phase identifier, ordered before every other Journal_Entry, with all of those blocks, including any level-1 heading, as the Journal_Entry's content, and with a title that is the text of the first of those blocks that is a level-1 Markdown heading (#) with text other than whitespace, cut to its first 200 characters, or the fixed title "Journal front matter" when no such block exists.
3. WHEN the Journal_Parser creates a Journal_Entry, THE Journal_Parser SHALL create, in source order, one Entry_Section for each top-level Markdown block in the Journal_Entry's content, except that each list yields one Entry_Section per list item, including everything nested inside the item, and each table yields one Entry_Section for the header row, including the dashed delimiter row below it, and one Entry_Section per body row.
4. IF the Journal_Parser cannot assign a span of source text that contains characters other than whitespace to a Phase_Heading or an Entry_Section, THEN THE Migration_Service SHALL import the span unchanged as a separate Not_Published Entry_Section at the span's source position within the Journal_Entry whose source lines include the span, and list the span's source line numbers and that Journal_Entry's title in the import report.
5. THE Journal_Printer SHALL format a sequence of Journal_Entries as journal Markdown in which each Journal_Entry appears in sequence order as a Phase_Heading, whose text is the Journal_Entry's heading text when the Journal_Entry has one, followed by all of the Journal_Entry's Entry_Sections in order, whatever each Entry_Section's Disclosure_Level, and a Journal_Entry without a phase identifier appears without a Phase_Heading.
6. WHEN the Journal_Parser parses the Journal_Printer output for the Journal_Entries that the Journal_Parser produced from one journal Markdown input, THE Journal_Parser SHALL produce Journal_Entries that match the original Journal_Entries one-to-one and in order, with the same phase identifiers, heading texts, and titles, and Entry_Sections that match each original Journal_Entry's Entry_Sections one-to-one and in order, with the same content character for character, including code spans and LaTeX-style math (round-trip property).
7. WHEN the Journal_Printer formats all Journal_Entries of a completed journal import in source order, before any Member edits them, THE Journal_Printer SHALL produce Markdown that renders to the same HTML as the source journal Markdown, where both are rendered with the same Markdown renderer and settings and the comparison treats each run of whitespace outside code blocks and code spans as one space and ignores leading and trailing whitespace.
8. WHEN a journal import completes, THE Migration_Service SHALL include in the import report the Journal_Printer output for the imported Journal_Entries as a Markdown file that Members can download, a statement of whether that output passes the comparison in criterion 7, and the title and source line range of each Journal_Entry whose printed Markdown fails the same comparison against the Journal_Entry's source lines.

8. THE Export_Service SHALL produce each Public_Archive_Export and Internal_Backup within the Workers and D1 free-plan limits and the R2 Class A and Class B operation limits listed in the Introduction.

11. WHILE the Public_Traffic of the current UTC day has used up the Public_Traffic_Budget of any one of the Workers request, D1 rows-read, and D1 rows-written daily allowances, THE Public_Site SHALL replace public search, the Outside_Submission_Form, and each form opened by a Response_Link with a notice stating that the feature is unavailable until 00:00 UTC.

### Requirement 30: Public Search

**User Story:** As an outside researcher, I want to search the public archive, so that I can find relevant findings, sources, and records without browsing every listing.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Visitor submits a search query on the Public_Site, THE Public_Site SHALL return, in pages of at most 20 results, every Research_Item and Attachment whose Public_View contains every term of the query, treating the Public_Views of the Entry_Sections, Replies, and Dissent_Notes whose Root_Item is a Research_Item, other than withdrawn Dissent_Notes (Requirement 42.8), as part of that Research_Item's Public_View and excluding the contents of Attachment files.
2. THE Public_Site SHALL match public search terms and exact phrases regardless of letter case and diacritics in either the query or the Public_View, so that "Schlussel" matches "Schlüssel" and "SCHLÜSSEL" matches "schlussel".
3. WHEN a Visitor submits a search query containing words enclosed in double quotation marks, THE Public_Site SHALL return only results whose Public_View contains each enclosed group of words consecutively and in the given order.
4. THE Public_Site SHALL allow public search results to be filtered by one or more kinds, chosen from the Research_Item kinds, Attachment, and Withheld, where every result whose Effective_Disclosure is Withheld has the kind Withheld, and by a date range with an optional inclusive start date and an optional inclusive end date applied to the date shown in each result, which for a Research_Item is the Item_Date.
5. THE Public_Site SHALL show each public search result whose Effective_Disclosure is Public with the result's kind, title, date (for a Research_Item, the Item_Date), Public_URL, the status that the Public_Site presents with the result if any, a withdrawal notice if the result is withdrawn, and an excerpt of at most 300 characters taken from the Public_View with the matched terms highlighted.

7. WHEN a change that removes material from a Public_View is saved, including a change of Effective_Disclosure from Public to Withheld, from Public to Not_Published, or from Withheld to Not_Published, a change of Public_Presentation from Full_Content to Summary_Only, the marking of an External_Party as Do_Not_Name, an edit that deletes text, and the permanent deletion of an Item, THE Public_Site SHALL stop matching and showing the removed material in public search, including in cached responses, within 5 minutes after the save.

9. THE Public_Site SHALL show each public search result whose Effective_Disclosure is Withheld with only the result's Disclosure_Line and Public_URL, highlighting the matched terms in the Disclosure_Line.
10. IF a search query term occurs in text that no Public_View presents, such as text of a Disclosure_Unit whose Effective_Disclosure is Not_Published, text of a Withheld Disclosure_Unit outside the Disclosure_Line, the content, Entry_Sections, and Replies of a Research_Item whose Public_Presentation is Summary_Only, or an External_Party name that the Public_Site replaces with a Generic_Label, THEN THE Public_Site SHALL return the same response that the Public_Site would return if that text did not exist.
11. IF a Visitor submits a search query that contains no letter or digit, contains more than 200 characters, or has a date range whose start date is later than its end date, THEN THE Public_Site SHALL return no results, show a message identifying each problem, and keep the query and filters in the search form.
12. THE Public_Site SHALL allow a Visitor to limit public search to one Public Project, with that limit set by default when the Visitor searches from that Project's Project_Section, or to search every Public Project together.
13. THE Public_Site SHALL show with each public search result whose Effective_Disclosure is Public the name of the result's Project.

20. WHEN an Administrator records a Contact_Review, THE Moderation_Service SHALL require the choice Keep, with a reason of 1 to 1,000 characters, or Erase; record the choice, the reason, the Administrator, and the time without changing earlier Contact_Reviews; for Erase, erase the Submitter_Contact within 24 hours; and in both cases keep the Submitter_Attribution and the submitted text.
21. WHILE an Outside_Submission has been due for Contact_Review for 30 days or more without a Contact_Review, THE Moderation_Service SHALL keep its Submitter_Contact, show it as overdue for Contact_Review in the Moderation_Queue, and THE Notification_Service SHALL repeat the Reminder of criterion 19 for it every 30 days until a Contact_Review is recorded.
22. THE Moderation_Service SHALL allow an Administrator to correct the Submitter_Attribution or the Submitter_Contact of any Outside_Submission, or erase its Submitter_Contact, at any time, recording the Administrator, the time, the previous values except an erased Submitter_Contact, and a reason of 1 to 1,000 characters, so that a correction or deletion request does not wait for a Contact_Review; a corrected Submitter_Attribution becomes the Credit of every Item created from the Outside_Submission.

### Requirement 37: Bulk Import from a Manifest

**User Story:** As a curator, I want to import a folder of files together with a spreadsheet that describes each item, so that my collected material enters the record in one checked step.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an Administrator starts a Bulk_Import with a folder of files and an Import_Manifest, THE Migration_Service SHALL read through the Manifest_Parser one Manifest_Row per item with the Row_Key, kind, title, date, Parties, Source fields (archive or collection, archival reference, Source_Kind, the person who recovered the Source, reliability rating, and reliability note), Rights_Status, rights holder, rights terms, Origin, the names of the row's files within the folder, the optional caption, crediting contributor (who becomes the Credit of each Attachment created from the row), and version marks, and the kind-specific values and optional Log_Summary that criterion 15 lists.
2. WHEN a Bulk_Import starts, THE Migration_Service SHALL, before creating any Item, validate that every Manifest_Row has a Row_Key, a kind, and a title; that no Row_Key appears in more than one Manifest_Row; that each kind is a Research_Item kind other than Journal_Entry; that each Rights_Status, Source_Kind, and reliability rating is a value of its set that has not been retired; that each Party name matches at most one Member or External_Party; and that each named file exists in the folder, is named by no other Manifest_Row, and is no larger than the Attachment_Size_Limit.
3. IF any Manifest_Row or named file fails validation, THEN THE Migration_Service SHALL create no Item and show the importing Administrator a validation report listing every error, including each error the Manifest_Parser returns, with the row number and the column where they apply, the file name where the error concerns a file, and the reason.
4. IF the folder of a Bulk_Import contains files that no Manifest_Row names, THEN THE Migration_Service SHALL import no Attachment for those files and list them as not imported in the import report and in any validation report, without treating them as validation errors.
5. THE Migration_Service SHALL set every Disclosure_Unit created by a Bulk_Import to Disclosure_Level Not_Published and Classification_State Unclassified, regardless of any value in the Import_Manifest.
6. WHEN the Migration_Service reaches a Manifest_Row whose Row_Key is identical, character for character, to the Row_Key of a Research_Item that an earlier or concurrently running Bulk_Import created, THE Migration_Service SHALL create no Item for the row, keep the existing Research_Item and the Research_Item's Attachments unchanged, including their Disclosure_Levels, Classification_States, and edits, and report the row as skipped.
7. WHEN the version marks of a Manifest_Row mark files of that row as successive versions of one another, THE Migration_Service SHALL import those files as Attachments in one Version_Chain in the order the version marks state.
8. IF the version marks in an Import_Manifest would form a branch or a cycle in a Version_Chain, or name a file that the same Manifest_Row does not name, THEN THE Migration_Service SHALL report each affected row as a validation error.
9. WHEN a file of a Bulk_Import is stored, THE Migration_Service SHALL verify that the stored file's size and checksum match the source file before creating the file's Attachment.
10. WHEN a Bulk_Import that created at least one Item completes or stops, THE Activity_Service SHALL treat the Items the Bulk_Import created as one Submission by the importing Administrator.
11. WHEN a Bulk_Import completes or stops, THE Migration_Service SHALL show the importing Administrator an import report listing the numbers of Research_Items and Attachments created, skipped, and failed, and the row number, Row_Key, and reason for each skipped or failed Manifest_Row.
12. WHEN every Manifest_Row and every named file of a Bulk_Import passes validation, THE Migration_Service SHALL create, for each Manifest_Row that is not skipped, one Research_Item of the row's kind and one Attachment for each file the row names, with that Research_Item as each Attachment's Parent, applying the row's title, date, Parties, Source fields, kind-specific values, and Log_Summary to the Research_Item as criterion 15 states, the row's Origin and caption to each Attachment, the row's crediting contributor, when given, as each Attachment's Credit, and the row's Rights_Status, rights holder, and rights terms to each Attachment and to a Source, with each Party name linked to the Member or External_Party of that name, or to a new External_Party when none has that name.
13. IF a file of a Manifest_Row fails to store or fails the size and checksum check, or the Bulk_Import stops before every Item of a Manifest_Row has been created, including when a platform daily limit is reached, THEN THE Migration_Service SHALL keep no Item created from that Manifest_Row, keep the Items of every other Manifest_Row, and report the row as failed with the reason and, where a file is concerned, the file name.
14. THE Research_CMS SHALL run each Bulk_Import so that, on every day the Bulk_Import runs, Member_Operations other than the Bulk_Import that are within the Expected_Usage_Profile complete without reaching a Workers or D1 free-plan daily limit.
15. THE Migration_Service SHALL read these optional kind-specific columns and apply each value to the Research_Item field of the same name: for a Finding row, claim and scope caveat, both required; for a Hypothesis row, statement, required; for a Run row, scope, parameters, tool name, tool version, compute resource, and Run_Outcome, all required, and end time, Resume_Point, and notes, optional; for a Correspondence row, direction (sent or received), required, and channel and content, optional; for an Archival_Request row, archive or desk, request text, and Archival_Request_Status, all required, and contact, channel, reply date, and reply content, optional; for a Topic row, body, required; and, for a row of any kind, an optional Log_Summary; with the date column giving a Source's document date, a Correspondence's date, an Archival_Request's date sent, and a Run's start time, and left empty in Finding, Hypothesis, and Topic rows.
16. WHEN a Bulk_Import starts, THE Migration_Service SHALL, before creating any Item, also validate that each Manifest_Row fills every column that criterion 15 requires for the row's kind and leaves empty each kind-specific column of the other kinds and, where criterion 15 says so, the date column; that the row's values pass every check that the Records_Service applies when the importing Administrator submits a Research_Item of the row's kind with those values, including the checks of Requirements 5.8, 6.6, 6.7, 7.1, 7.8, 7.9, 8.9, 9.5, 10.6, 10.7, and 16.9, except that the Log_Summary is checked only for a length of at most 2,000 characters, so that a row of any kind may leave the Log_Summary empty (Requirements 41.1 and 41.2); that the archive or desk and the contact each match at most one Member or External_Party; and that each row that names files has an Origin of 1 to 5,000 characters.
17. WHEN an Administrator starts a Bulk_Import, THE Migration_Service SHALL require the Administrator to choose one Project and create every Research_Item of the Bulk_Import in that Project.
18. THE Migration_Service SHALL create each Finding and each Hypothesis of a Bulk_Import with the Finding_Status or Hypothesis_Status Open.

### Requirement 38: Import Manifest Parsing and Printing

**User Story:** As a curator, I want the import spreadsheet read exactly as I wrote it, and checkable by printing it back, so that no item, value, or accented character is lost or altered.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Manifest_Parser receives an Import_Manifest in XLSX format or in CSV format encoded in UTF-8 with or without a byte order mark, THE Manifest_Parser SHALL take the first row (for XLSX, of the first worksheet) as the header row and produce one Manifest_Row for each later row that has at least one non-empty cell, in source order, with each value assigned to the column named above it in the header row, including columns that a Bulk_Import does not use.
2. IF the header row of an Import_Manifest lacks a column for the Row_Key, kind, title, date, Parties, Source fields, Rights_Status, rights holder, rights terms, Origin, or file names, names a column more than once, or leaves unnamed a column that holds a non-empty value, THEN THE Manifest_Parser SHALL return no Manifest_Rows and one error for each missing, repeated, or unnamed column, identifying an unnamed column by its position.
3. THE Manifest_Parser SHALL read each value as exactly the characters of the source cell, which for an XLSX cell means the cell's value as formatted by the cell's number format, such as a date in its displayed format, with diacritics, letter case, leading and trailing spaces, and line breaks inside quoted CSV values unchanged.
4. THE Manifest_Printer SHALL format a set of Manifest_Rows as a CSV Import_Manifest encoded in UTF-8 with a byte order mark, with a header row naming the columns in their original order, followed by one row per Manifest_Row in the order of the set.
5. WHEN the Manifest_Printer output for a set of Manifest_Rows produced by the Manifest_Parser is parsed by the Manifest_Parser, THE Manifest_Parser SHALL produce the same columns in the same order and the same number of Manifest_Rows in the same order, with every value the same sequence of characters as in the original set (round-trip property).
6. IF a Manifest_Row has an empty value in the Row_Key column, has the same Row_Key as another Manifest_Row of the same Import_Manifest, or has a value that the corresponding Item field does not allow, such as a kind that is not a Research_Item kind or a Rights_Status that is not one of the current Rights_Status values, THEN THE Manifest_Parser SHALL return no Manifest_Rows and one error for each such value, naming the row number that a spreadsheet application shows for the row, the column, and the reason.
7. IF a CSV Import_Manifest contains a byte sequence that is not valid UTF-8 or a quoted value that is never closed, or an XLSX Import_Manifest cannot be opened as a workbook, THEN THE Manifest_Parser SHALL return no Manifest_Rows and an error indicating the problem and, for a CSV Import_Manifest, the row where the first such problem starts.
8. WHEN the Manifest_Parser reads an XLSX Import_Manifest in which a worksheet other than the first contains a non-empty cell, THE Migration_Service SHALL list each such worksheet by name in the validation report as not imported.
9. IF an Import_Manifest has a Manifest_Row of a kind for which Requirement 37.15 requires a column that the header row lacks, THEN THE Manifest_Parser SHALL return no Manifest_Rows and one error for each missing column, naming the column and the kind that requires it.

12. IF the AI_Service returns an error or no text, or no AI_Draft arrives within 30 seconds of the request, THEN THE Research_CMS SHALL show a notice that no AI_Draft was produced, discard any AI_Draft for that request that arrives later, leave the text in the editor unchanged, and keep the Member's unsaved input.
13. THE Research_CMS SHALL offer AI_Draft requests only to authenticated active Members and only for the Log_Summaries, Public_Summaries, and Disclosure_Lines for which the Member's Role grants the Permission to request an AI_Draft, and send text to the AI_Service only in answer to such a request, so that no Public_Traffic, import, or restore causes any use of the AI_Service.

15. IF an Administrator sets the AI_Input_Limit to a value other than a whole number of characters from 1,000 to 50,000, THEN THE Research_CMS SHALL reject the value, keep the previous AI_Input_Limit, and show the allowed range.

18. IF the Research_CMS receives an AI_Draft request for which the text that criterion 1 or 2 selects is empty or whitespace-only, THEN THE Research_CMS SHALL send no text to the AI_Service, show a notice that there is no text to draft from, and leave each field being drafted unchanged.

### Requirement 49: Two-Administrator Approvals

2. WHEN an active Administrator proposes an action that criterion 1 lists, THE Research_CMS SHALL record the proposal with the action and its exact effect, the Administrator, the time, and a reason of 1 to 2,000 characters, show it to every active Administrator in a list of open Dual_Approvals, and create a Review_Requested Notification_Event for every other active Administrator.

WHEN an active Administrator other than the proposing Administrator approves an open Dual_Approval with a reason of 1 to 2,000 characters, THE Research_CMS SHALL apply the proposed action in the same save, record the approving Administrator, the time, and the reason, and list the action in the Activity_Feed as one entry.

An Attachment that drops to Withheld because of its rights gets a Disclosure_Line with only the file kind as the kind of material and the upload date as the date (Requirement 33.5).
14. **Party naming:** Do_Not_Name is off by default, and the publication preview shows each Party name before anything is published. The flag covers structured fields such as Disclosure_Lines and Party listings, not names typed into free text.
15. **Outside submissions:** there is no email-confirmation step; the per-address limits restrict misuse of the acknowledgement email. Outside files are not virus-scanned, because the platform has no free scanning option, so Members should treat them as untrusted; Requirement 14.6 stops files from running scripts in the logged-in app. Accepted material is credited publicly with the name and affiliation the Submitter gave. The Submitter answers requests for more information through a Response_Link.
16. **Bulk import:** a Bulk_Import counts as one Submission, so other Members get New_Flags on the imported items and one Notification for the whole import. The journal migration keeps its rule of no flags and no notifications, because the team already knows that content. Each Manifest_Row carries a Row_Key, such as a catalogue number, and re-runs match rows by Row_Key. Caption, crediting contributor, and version marks (which file supersedes which) are optional columns. Journal_Entry is not an allowed row kind, and each Party name in a manifest links to the existing Member or External_Party of the same name, or to a new External_Party when none has that name (Requirements 37.2 and 37.12).
17. **Migration details:** migrated ledger rows stay as Entry_Sections and are not converted into Findings automatically. Quote and paraphrase wording is kept exactly as written, and structured provenance labels are applied by hand afterwards. Migrated Journal_Entries use their phase start dates as Item_Date, while the date filters of Member search use creation dates, which for migrated content are the import date (Requirements 21.5 and 26.1).
18. **Public journal during migration:** none of the migrated content appears on the Public_Site until an Administrator classifies it. The existing journal site at helut.org is offline (confirmed by the campaign owner in October 2026), so the journal has no other public copy during migration, and the old site needs no withdrawal or redirect step.
19. **Exports:** the scheduled Public_Archive_Export runs on the first day of each month. A single free-plan Worker request cannot package hundreds of megabytes, so the design assembles packages in parts, for example by writing them to the Object_Store piece by piece or by assembling them in the Administrator's browser. Restore (Requirement 36.4 and 36.5) is included because a backup is only useful if it can be restored; say if a documented manual restore is enough.
20. **Public traffic:** every request that reaches a Worker counts against the daily limit shared with Member operations. The design will therefore likely serve public pages and the public search index as static files and stop excess form requests at the network edge before they reach a Worker (Requirement 28.5 to 28.7).
21. **When a Log_Summary is required:** a Log_Summary is required when a Member submits a Research_Item of any kind except Journal_Entry and Topic, including when an Administrator accepts an Outside_Submission (Requirement 41.2). It is optional for a Journal_Entry, which is itself a log entry, and for a Topic, which is a general discussion. Research_Items created by the journal migration, a Bulk_Import (whose Log_Summary column is optional), or a restore can lack one, and the Project_Log then states that none is recorded. Once written, a Log_Summary can be changed but not removed, so a Dissent_Note always has a summary to sit next to (Requirement 41.5).
22. **Who the author of a Log_Summary is:** the Research_Item's Owner, which for imported material is the importing Administrator and for accepted outside material the accepting Administrator. Administrators can still withdraw, permanently delete, or change the disclosure of the item, but cannot reword its Log_Summary, and a Restoration by an Administrator who is not the Owner leaves the Log_Summary unchanged (Requirements 2.5 and 22.5). If the Owner's access is revoked, the wording stays as it was last saved.
23. **Dissent_Notes:** each Member can have one current Dissent_Note per Log_Summary and edits it to update it. A Dissent_Note starts Not_Published and appears on the Public_Site only when the item is presented with Full_Content and an Administrator sets the Dissent_Note itself to Public; the publication preview lists every Dissent_Note that would not be published (Requirement 23.16). A withdrawn Dissent_Note stays on the item's page and in its history but no longer appears next to the Log_Summary elsewhere. A Dissent_Note written about wording that has since changed is marked as such. An Administrator can permanently delete a Dissent_Note, for example one that contains material that must not be kept; the deletion leaves a visible record and notifies both authors. The new Event_Type Dissent tells the Log_Summary's author about each new, changed, withdrawn, or deleted Dissent_Note (Requirement 42.4) and starts, like every Event_Type, with in-app On and email Immediate; other Members see each Dissent_Note event in the Activity_Feed instead of a notification.
24. **Group_Notes in email (confirm):** emails to Members carry only Public_View content (Requirement 20.6), and a Group_Note starts Not_Published, so the email about a new submission links to the Group_Note but does not contain its text (Requirement 41.12). The option is to include the Group_Note text in emails for Members who opt in; the cost is that the text then passes through the Email_Provider and recipients' mailboxes. The same rule applies to Log_Summaries and Dissent_Notes: an email shows them only as far as their Public_Views go, so an email shows a Log_Summary only when the Public_Site presents it, and then with the Dissent_Notes the Public_Site presents with it (Requirement 42.15).
25. **Project_Log order:** entries are ordered by Log_Date, which is the Item_Date except for Sources. A Source's Item_Date is its document date, which can be decades old and would misplace the Source in a log of the team's work, so a Source is logged on its creation date. The public Project_Log shows a Log_Summary only under Full_Content and the Public_Summary under Summary_Only (Requirement 40.5 and 40.6).
26. **Projects (confirm):** every Member sees every Project, though individual Research_Items in it can be Restricted (decision 3); per-Project membership is a possible later addition through the Role_Permission_Map. Only Administrators create Projects, change their names, descriptions, Project_Slugs, featured flags, and order, change their disclosure, and move items between them. An Administrator creates the P1030680 Project before the journal migration, which imports into it (Requirement 26.1). A new Project starts Not_Published, so the P1030680 Project must be set to Public before anything in it can appear publicly. A Withheld Project shows only its Disclosure_Line. A Project_Slug can be corrected until the Project first becomes Public or Withheld and is then fixed. Projects cannot be deleted in this revision. Item Public_URLs do not contain the Project (Requirement 29.8), so moving an item to another Project keeps its address and citations.
27. **Muting a Project (confirm):** each Member can mute any Project. Muting stops New_Submission, Reply, and ordinary Item_Update notifications for that Project. Mentions, review requests, Dissent notifications, Reminders, Outside_Submission notifications, System_Alerts, and the disclosure alerts sent to Administrators still arrive, New_Flags and Updated_Flags are still set, and the Project's Activity_Feed entries are still shown (Requirements 39.9 and 39.17).
28. **AI drafting (in scope as an optional feature, confirm):** AI drafting is included because the expected 50 drafts per day use only part of the free allocation: about 950 Neurons on an 8B model or about 5,900 on a 70B model, of 10,000. The Research_CMS never requests Neurons beyond the daily free allocation, so nothing is charged on either Workers plan (Requirement 28.14). The AI_Drafting_Setting starts On, and an Administrator can turn it off. When a Member asks for a draft, the item's text, which may be Not_Published, is processed by Workers AI inside the team's Cloudflare account; Cloudflare states that it does not train models on that content or make it available to other customers. The model is chosen in design. A draft of a Disclosure_Line covers only the kind of material and the note, because a draft written from withheld content can repeat that content; the publication preview remains the check (Requirement 23.6). The Revision history records which saved text began as an AI_Draft and which model wrote it (Requirement 44.6), but the Public_Site does not mark AI-assisted text; say if public pages should state it.
29. **Bulk import of every kind:** each Research_Item kind except Journal_Entry has columns for the fields its requirement makes mandatory, filled only in rows of that kind (Requirement 37.15 and 37.16). Topic rows are allowed and need a body. Findings and Hypotheses are imported as Open (Requirement 37.18): every Hypothesis starts Open (Requirement 8.1), and the statuses Confirmed and Eliminated need an Evidence_Link (Requirements 5.3 and 8.2) that a manifest row cannot express, so the manifest has no status or Elimination_Scope columns and those statuses are set by hand after import. A value in another kind's column, or a date in a Finding, Hypothesis, or Topic row, is a validation error, so that no value is silently dropped.
30. **The front-matter journal entry:** the journal entry built from the blocks before the first Phase_Heading has no phase identifier, so re-imports recognise it by the fixed import key "front-matter" (Requirement 26.10).
31. **No donations (confirm):** ResearchForce accepts no donations and runs no fundraising. The About page says so, and the Public_Site has no donation, payment, or sponsorship feature (Requirement 46.1 and 46.3). If this changes, the fixed status text and criterion 46.3 change with it.
32. **Group status text fixed in design:** the status section of the About page states that ResearchForce is an informal, independent, unincorporated group; that it is not a company, a charity, or a registered non-profit and is not run for profit; that it accepts no donations and runs no fundraising; and that it has no institutional affiliation except where a page says so (Requirement 46.1). In line with that, no Public_Site page asks for money or offers a way to give or pay it (Requirement 46.3). The status section and the Group_Statement in the footer are fixed in design, so that no Workspace edit can drop or alter them by mistake. Changing the group's status, for example if it incorporates or decides to accept donations, is a design change. The footer's copyright statement and the fixed statements on the People, Licence, and Corrections pages are also fixed in design (Requirements 46.2, 46.6, 46.15, and 46.21). The rest of each Policy_Page is either generated content, such as the Public_Profiles, the list of Methods_Terms, and the Retention_Table, or Administrator-maintained text in a fixed set of Policy_Sections (Requirement 46.32).
33. **Public profiles off by default:** each Member decides whether the People page shows the Member, and only the Member can change that (Requirements 3.2 and 46.4). A Public_Profile shows exactly the display name, Specialties, affiliations, and short biography, all or nothing; a Member who wants a shorter public profile shortens those fields or keeps the Public_Profile Off. Revoked Members leave the People page within 5 minutes. Public Credits still show display names whether or not a Public_Profile is On (decision 17).
34. **Contact only through the form:** no email address, telephone number, or postal address of the group or of any Member is published. This includes the Responsible_Person, whom the Privacy page names together with a link to the form (Requirement 46.35). The Sending_Address appears only as the sender of emails, and replies to it are not read (assumption 47). Registered Member email addresses that appear in published text are replaced by a placeholder, and the publication preview shows where (Requirement 46.9). The General_Choice lets a Visitor write about the group as a whole instead of one Project (Requirement 31.13). While the Public_Traffic_Budget is used up, the form, and so the way to send a Removal_Request, is unavailable until 00:00 UTC (Requirement 28.11); this delay is small against the 14-day Response_Target.
35. **Removal requests (target to confirm):** a Removal_Request covers removal of material and requests to see, correct, or erase personal data. The form asks for no licence agreement for it, and accepting one creates no Item. The aim is a first Moderation_Decision within 14 days of receipt for every Correction and Removal_Request, with Reminders to Administrators after 10 days, after 14 days, and weekly after that (Requirement 46.24 and 46.25). The 14 days is fixed, not a setting, because the fixed text on the Corrections page and in acknowledgements states it. After an acceptance, the request's files are deleted within 24 hours and the requester's email address is erased after 90 to 91 days, as for a rejection; the request text and the decision stay as the record of what was done. Every Member can read Removal_Requests, consistent with decision 3.
36. **Licence agreement by Members:** copyright stays with the credited authors, so the Licence page's statement that they license their published work under the Content_Licence is true only if each author has agreed. Each Member therefore records a Licence_Agreement once, and an Administrator cannot newly publish work credited to a Member without one, in the same way as the rights and consent gates (Requirements 33.4, 34.2, and 46.17). Submitters already agree on the form (Requirement 31.1). Licences such as CC BY cannot be revoked for copies already distributed, so a Licence_Agreement cannot be withdrawn once given, and the Research_CMS offers no way to withdraw one (Requirement 46.16); a Member who wants work taken down asks an Administrator to change its disclosure. If the Content_Licence changes, Members agree again before more of their work is published.
37. **Analytics:** visitor analytics are optional. If used, they are cookieless and aggregate only, with Cloudflare Web Analytics as the free candidate, and the Privacy page names the service (Requirement 45.19). Acceptance tests of Requirement 45.8 use lab measurements under the Reference_Mobile_Profile; Core Web Vitals reported by the analytics service, if any, serve as ongoing monitoring.
38. **No cookies or browser storage on the Public_Site:** only the Bot_Check on form pages and the unsent-input safeguard of Requirement 28.10 may store anything in a Visitor's browser (Requirement 45.18). Cloudflare features that set cookies on every page, such as the bot-management cookie of Bot Fight Mode, stay off for Public_Site addresses. Fonts are served from the Research_CMS's own addresses, because externally hosted fonts send Visitor data to a third party (Requirement 45.17 and 45.20).
39. **Third-party components and accessibility:** the Identity_Provider's login pages are not pages of the Research_CMS and fall outside Requirement 45.5. The Bot_Check runs inside the form pages, so the design checks that the Turnstile mode used conforms to WCAG 2.2 Level AA; Turnstile can run without a visible puzzle. The content of Attachment files, such as scanned documents, is outside the conformance claim.
40. **Language and dates:** the interface is in English, and dates are written with English month names, such as 12 May 1943 (Requirement 45.12). Passages in other languages, such as German quotations, need a language mark for WCAG 2.2 Level AA (success criterion 3.1.2), so Members can mark a span's language (Requirement 45.6). Imported text has no language marks until Members add them.
41. **Quotation marks:** the Research_CMS uses typographic quotation marks in its own text but shows Member, Submitter, and imported text exactly as stored, with no automatic conversion of straight quotation marks, because transcriptions and Quotes must keep the source's characters (Requirement 45.14).
42. **Visual identity:** decided in design. The existing P1030680 journal site's editorial style, with a variable serif typeface (Fraunces) and an editorial layout of kicker, lede, status strip, and attachment lists with credits, is the natural starting point. A Project_Accent is chosen from the Design_System's accent colours, each checked for contrast in both colour schemes, so that no Project can break Requirement 45.5.
43. **Methods page:** statuses, ratings, rights values, relation labels, label kinds, and the terms Withheld, Scope_Caveat, Elimination_Scope, Resume_Point, withdrawn, and superseded are explained on the Methods page from Public_Descriptions. Initial descriptions are written in design, and each value an Administrator adds needs one (Requirement 46.12 and 46.13). Public pages link each such term to its explanation (Requirement 46.14).
44. **Policy pages in exports:** each Public_Archive_Export includes the About, Methods, and Licence pages, which a reader of the archive needs to interpret and reuse it (Requirement 35.12). The People and Privacy pages describe the live site and current personal data, so exports leave them out. Internal_Backups include every Policy_Section with its Revisions (Requirement 36.11).
45. **Free plan (Requirement 28 re-checked):** the Policy_Pages, the People page, metadata, print styles, fonts, and other Design_System assets are stored files or cached responses, so they cause no D1 queries, and the assets cause no Worker requests (Requirement 28.16). Metadata and print styles add no requests of their own. The analytics beacon, if used, reports to Cloudflare and not to a Worker. Public page views keep counting against the Public_Traffic_Budget only where a page goes through a Worker, as before (Requirement 28.5).
46. **Privacy page retention values:** the Retention_Table is generated from the values in this document, so the Privacy page cannot drift from what the Research_CMS does (Requirement 46.19). Network addresses used for submission limits are kept at most 24 hours (Requirement 46.20). The retention of accepted Submitters' details remains open question 3, and the Retention_Table states the assumption in force.
47. **Sending email through Microsoft 365 (design notes):** the design registers an app in the campaign owner's tenant with the Microsoft Graph Mail.Send application permission and restricts that permission to the one sending mailbox through Exchange Online RBAC for Applications, so that the credentials cannot send as any other mailbox or read any mail (Requirement 20.19). The sending mailbox has the display name ResearchForce (Requirement 45.21), is assumed to need no paid licence of its own, for example as a shared mailbox (if it needs one, the licence counts as an Email_Provider charge under Requirement 28.4), and keeps no copies of sent mail (Requirement 20.24). Replies to the Sending_Address are not read: the design either rejects incoming mail at that mailbox or deletes it after a short period, and the emails tell recipients how to respond instead, through a Response_Link or the Outside_Submission_Form for Submitters and through the Workspace for Members. The Recipient_Cap (Requirement 20.16) is the Research_CMS's own safety cap. Its initial value of 2,000 recipients per 24 hours is well below the mailbox's 10,000-recipient limit, and the Recipient_Cap must also stay below the tenant's shared external limit (TERRL), which scales with the tenant's number of licences, is shared with the campaign owner's other mail, and for a small tenant can be low. Before launch, the campaign owner therefore checks the tenant's shared external limit in the Exchange admin center's "Tenant Outbound External Recipients" report and sets the Recipient_Cap below it, leaving room for the tenant's other mail; an Administrator can change the Recipient_Cap later within 1 to 10,000 (Requirement 20.25). The planned load of about 140 emails per day is under 10% of the initial Recipient_Cap. The Outside_Submission_Limits keep acknowledgements to addresses that Visitors type in to 20 per day, so the public form cannot turn the mailbox into a bulk sender. If Microsoft restricts the mailbox for suspected spam, every email fails until an administrator of the tenant lifts the restriction, and the in-app System_Alerts of Requirements 20.10 and 32.12 report the failures. Client secrets and certificates expire, so an Administrator replaces the credentials before they expire (Requirement 20.21). Microsoft 365 keeps its own delivery records, such as message traces, outside the Research_CMS, and the Privacy page's Policy_Section "Services that process data" names Microsoft 365 as a service that processes email addresses.
48. **Email authentication for researchforce.org (design notes):** Microsoft's servers send the mail, so SPF for researchforce.org is the single record "v=spf1 include:spf.protection.outlook.com -all", with no Cloudflare include. DKIM signing is enabled for researchforce.org in Microsoft 365, so that each email carries a DKIM signature for researchforce.org instead of the tenant's onmicrosoft.com domain. The DMARC policy starts at p=none and moves to p=quarantine once the aggregate reports show that only correctly signed mail from the Sending_Address uses the domain (Requirement 20.22 and 20.23). The reports go to an address that the design chooses, not to the Sending_Address. Any other service that later sends mail as researchforce.org is added to the SPF record and signs with DKIM for the domain before it sends.

The swap is faster than the 15 minutes allowed for other public additions, because removing the previous name falls under the 5-minute rule and a gap between removal and addition would leave the page naming nobody. The Research_CMS does not ask the designated Member to accept; the group agrees who takes the role before an Administrator records it. The Privacy page names the Responsible_Person whether or not that Member's Public_Profile is On. Removal_Requests reach the Responsible_Person through the existing Outside_Submission Event_Type, so no new Event_Type is needed, the Responsible_Person's Notification_Preferences for that type apply, and a Responsible_Person who is also an Administrator gets one notification, not two (Requirement 46.36). The Reminders about overdue Removal_Requests still go to Administrators only (Requirement 46.25). If the Responsible_Person's access is revoked, the Privacy page keeps naming that Member until an Administrator designates another, so that the page always names someone, and every Administrator receives a System_Alert within 15 minutes and every 7 days until then (Requirement 46.40). The designation is a workspace-wide setting, so Internal_Backups include it (Requirement 36.1).
50. **Project accents (Requirement 45.22):** only Administrators set or clear a Project_Accent, and only one of the Design_System's accent colours is accepted, so that no Project can lower contrast below what Requirement 45.5 requires.
